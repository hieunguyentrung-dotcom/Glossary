Heating Systems / Hệ thống sưởi

14 ▾
English TermTiếng ViệtDefinition / Explanation
1climate-control systemhệ thống điều hòa khí hậuSystem that heats, cools and regulates indoor temperature using three components: source, distribution and control.
2thermostatbộ điều nhiệtDevice that automatically senses and regulates temperature in a heating or cooling system.
3central heating systemhệ thống sưởi trung tâmSingle system using a furnace or boiler to heat an entire building via radiators and hot water pipes.
4furnacelò sưởiDevice that burns fuel (gas, oil or electricity) to produce heat in a central heating system.
5boilernồi hơiDevice that heats water for circulation through radiators or underfloor pipes in a building.
6radiatorbộ tản nhiệtUnit that receives hot water from the boiler and releases heat into a room by radiation and convection.
7electric heat pumpmáy bơm nhiệt điệnDevice that moves heat between indoor and outdoor environments; can also function as an air conditioner.
8radiant skirting board heaterthiết bị sưởi chân tường bức xạLong metal heating unit with electrical elements installed along the base of walls.
9radiant ceiling/floor systemhệ thống sưởi bức xạ trần/sànHeating system embedded in floors, ceilings or walls that warms objects by radiation.
10hydronic heatinghệ thống sưởi bằng nước nóng tuần hoànSystem using a boiler to circulate hot water through tubes under the floor or in wall units.
11portable space heatermáy sưởi di độngFreestanding or wall-mounted heater for individual rooms; works with electricity, gas or kerosene.
12fuelnhiên liệuSubstance burned to produce energy in a heating system, e.g. gas, oil or electricity.
13kerosenedầu hỏaLiquid fuel used in some portable space heaters and cooking appliances.
14drivewaylối xe vào nhàPaved area leading to a building; sometimes heated by hydronic systems to prevent ice accumulation.

Solar Energy Systems / Hệ thống năng lượng mặt trời

12 ▾
English TermTiếng ViệtDefinition / Explanation
1solar energynăng lượng mặt trờiElectricity or heat produced from sunlight captured by solar panels.
2solar paneltấm pin mặt trờiDevice installed on roofs or walls to capture sunlight and generate electricity or heat water.
3solar photovoltaic (PV) systemhệ thống quang điện mặt trờiConverts sunlight into electricity using cells containing semiconductor materials.
4solar thermal systemhệ thống nhiệt mặt trờiUses sunlight to heat water for domestic use; panels face the sun and contain flowing water.
5semiconductorchất bán dẫnMaterial in PV cells whose electrons react with sunlight to produce an electric current.
6thermal collectorbộ thu nhiệtComponent inside solar thermal panels that absorbs solar heat to warm circulating water.
7hot water cylinderbình chứa nước nóngInsulated tank that stores hot water heated by solar thermal collectors.
8natural convectionđối lưu tự nhiênMovement of water through the solar system driven by temperature differences, without a pump.
9storage tankbồn chứaVessel mounted above or below solar collectors on the roof to store heated water.
10conventional boilernồi hơi truyền thốngBackup boiler used to heat water further or provide hot water when solar energy is insufficient.
11renewable energynăng lượng tái tạoEnergy from naturally replenished sources such as sunlight, wind or water.
12environmental pollutionô nhiễm môi trườngContamination of air, water or land by harmful substances; solar energy creates less of this.

Ventilation & Air Conditioning / Thông gió & Điều hòa

24 ▾
English TermTiếng ViệtDefinition / Explanation
1ventilationthông gióExchange of indoor air with outdoor air to reduce moisture, odours and pollutants.
2indoor airkhông khí trong nhàAir contained within a building, which may accumulate pollutants without ventilation.
3outdoor airkhông khí ngoài trờiFresh air from outside a building supplied to replace stale indoor air.
4moistuređộ ẩm / hơi ẩmWater vapour present in indoor air; excess moisture can cause physical damage to a building.
5odourmùi hôiUnpleasant smell in indoor air; removed by ventilation systems.
6pollutantchất ô nhiễmHarmful substance in indoor air such as VOCs, radon, or combustion products.
7VOChợp chất hữu cơ dễ bay hơiVolatile Organic Compound; harmful gas released from building materials and furniture.
8radonkhí radonNaturally occurring radioactive gas that can accumulate dangerously in poorly ventilated homes.
9humidityđộ ẩm không khíAmount of water vapour in indoor air; high levels must be controlled to prevent building damage.
10natural ventilationthông gió tự nhiênUncontrolled air movement through cracks, holes and vents such as doors and windows.
11infiltrationsự thẩm thấu không khíUncontrolled movement of air into a building through gaps in the building envelope.
12spot ventilationthông gió cục bộLocalised fans in rooms where contaminants are generated, e.g. kitchen extractor fans and bathroom fans.
13whole-house ventilationthông gió toàn bộ ngôi nhàSystem using fans and ducts to exhaust stale air and supply fresh air throughout the building.
14ductống dẫn gióChannel used to distribute or exhaust air in a whole-house ventilation system.
15stale airkhông khí tù đọngUsed, polluted indoor air that needs to be exhausted and replaced with fresh air.
16fresh airkhông khí tươiClean outdoor air supplied to a building to replace stale indoor air.
17exhaust ventilationthông gió hútVentilation system that forces stale air out of the building.
18supply ventilationthông gió cấpVentilation system that forces fresh outdoor air into the building.
19balanced ventilationthông gió cân bằngVentilation where the volume of air supplied and exhausted are equal.
20heat exchangerbộ trao đổi nhiệtDevice that recovers heat from exhaust air using hollow tubes and fins to warm incoming fresh air.
21trickle ventilatorcửa thông gió nhỏSmall adjustable vent fitted to windows or walls to allow controlled air flow.
22cross ventilationthông gió chéoVentilation achieved by placing openings on opposite sides of a room to create air flow.
23extractor fanquạt hútFan that removes contaminated air directly from a kitchen or bathroom.
24moisture control switchcông tắc kiểm soát độ ẩmSensor that automatically activates an extractor fan when indoor humidity rises above a set level.

10 ▾
English TermTiếng ViệtDefinition / Explanation

3thermal insulationcách nhiệtMaterial that reduces heat transfer through walls, roofs and floors of a building.
4thermal masskhối nhiệtDense material (concrete, stone) that absorbs and stores heat during the day, releasing it at night.
5shadingche nắngOverhangs, blinds or other devices that prevent excessive solar heat gain in summer.
6energy conservationtiết kiệm năng lượngReducing the amount of energy used in buildings through design and behaviour changes.
7renewable resourcetài nguyên tái tạoResource naturally replenished by the environment, such as sunlight, wind or water.
8emissionskhí thảiGases released into the atmosphere from burning fossil fuels; reduced by energy conservation.
9pledgecam kếtFormal promise to achieve an energy or environmental target, e.g. EU 2006 energy pledge.
10programmechương trìnhOrganised plan to promote energy efficiency, e.g. the EU SAVE Programme.
